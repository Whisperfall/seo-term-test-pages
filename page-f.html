Station Log F

The research station sits at the end of a gravel road, a few hours from the nearest town. Each morning started with coffee, a weather check and a short briefing on the day's route. Volunteers rotated every two weeks, so the notebooks show many different handwriting styles. We kept the instruments in waterproof cases and checked their batteries every night. Data were copied to two laptops and a paper log, because the internet connection often failed. Some days were spent indoors, sorting samples and labeling bottles for the lab.
